# 🧘 BreakFree

**BreakFree** is a simple and modern Android app designed to help users track their progress while quitting bad habits and building self-discipline.

The app focuses on one simple idea: **track your progress, stay consistent, and break free.**

> **Break the Habit. Build Yourself. 💪**

---

## ✨ Features

* 📅 **Day/Streak Counter** — Track how many days you have stayed consistent.
* 🗓️ **Manual Start Date** — Set or change your starting date manually.
* 🔄 **Reset Streak** — Easily restart your progress whenever needed.
* 🏆 **Achievement Ranks** — Unlock different milestones as your streak grows.
* 📊 **Progress up to 500 Days** — Track long-term progress and achievements.
* 📜 **History** — Keep previous streak records in a simple list.
* 🗑️ **Clear History** — Remove saved history when needed.
* 📢 **Banner Advertisement** — Includes an AdMob banner ad.
* ℹ️ **About Section** — Includes information about the app and its creator.
* 📱 **Responsive UI** — Designed to work across different Android screen sizes.
